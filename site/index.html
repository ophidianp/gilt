Built for practice

Learn how orders work. For free.

gilt behaves like a real brokerage account, so what you learn carries over: fills, buying power, waiting orders and the market's hours.

Fills

The real price, right now

Market orders fill at the live price. A limit buy fills at your price or lower, a stop sell once the price falls to your stop.

Buying power

Cash is held back for waiting orders

Place a limit buy and its cost is set aside, so you can't spend the same dollar twice. Sell orders hold their shares the same way.

Market hours

Closed means closed

Order after 4 PM and a market order waits for the next open, like it would at a broker. Crypto trades around the clock.

Shares or dollars

Buy $500 of anything

Enter shares, or a dollar amount and gilt works out the fractional shares. Max fills in everything you can afford.

Results

See how you'd really do

Open and closed P/L, how many of your sells were winners, and your account's value over time, recorded every minute gilt is open.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

Is any of the money real?

No. Your starting cash, every trade and every gain or loss only exist inside gilt on your PC. You can't deposit, withdraw or lose real money, and gilt never asks for a bank or brokerage login.

Are the prices real?

Yes. gilt reads live quotes from Yahoo Finance's public market data every 3 seconds while the US market is open. Orders fill at that price, with no fees.

What happens when the market is closed?

Market orders wait and fill at the next open. Limit and stop orders wait until the price is reached during market hours. Crypto trades any time.

Will my orders fill if gilt is closed?

They fill the next time gilt is open and sees the price reach your limit or stop. gilt has no servers, so nothing runs while it's closed.

Is gilt investment advice?

No. It's a place to practice. Results with pretend money, no fees and instant fills will differ from real trading.
